# Code of Conduct

We're a small community of people who miss a game. Let's keep it a place people want to come back to.

## Please do

- Be patient. Plenty of testers aren't developers, and plenty of us don't speak English as a first language.
- Argue about ideas and code, not about people.
- Ask before you assume the worst.
- Accept that the maintainers make the final call on what gets merged and how the project runs.

## Don't

- harass, insult or threaten anyone;
- use slurs or discriminate against anyone;
- post sexual content or push unwanted attention on someone;
- share someone's private details (real name, address, account info, IP address) without their OK;
- post game files, pirated stuff or links to them;
- advertise servers, sell accounts or items, or spam;
- set out to wreck threads or other people's servers.

## Where this applies

The GitHub repo (issues, pull requests, discussions), the forum, and any chat we run. It also applies when you're speaking for the project somewhere else.

## Reporting a problem

Send one of the maintainers a private message on the forum, or use the private report described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). We keep reports confidential.

## What we'll do

Depending on how bad it is and whether it keeps happening: a warning, edited or removed posts, a locked thread, or a temporary or permanent ban from the project's spaces.

Based on the ideas in the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
